## v1.0.1

The first release.

- Radial menus in the style of the game's ping wheel, one keybind per menu. Tap the keybind to use
  the quick action without the menu appearing.
- Menus can hold spells, items, toys, mounts, battle pets, macros, emotes, pet commands, equipment
  sets, action bars, and target and world markers.
- Works in combat. Actions you cannot use right now are hidden automatically, and icons show
  cooldowns, charges, range, and procs.
- Special actions: Close Menu, Last Used Action, and First Action.
- Submenus, scrolled through or spread into the menu.
- Library menus that fill themselves: consumables, quest items, hearthstones, gear, equipment sets,
  action bars, professions, specializations, and talent loadouts.
- Menus open at your cursor, or at a fixed position placed in Edit Mode.
- Share menus with a single string, with their submenus and macros.
- Controller support with ConsolePort and in WoW Forever's gamepad UI.
- Retail and WoW Forever.
